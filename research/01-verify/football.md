# Verify: football (soccer on the Betfair Exchange)

Date: 2026-11-15
Agent: docs-verifier

## Summary (max 5 lines)

Confirmed at the Betfair developer docs: in-play delay is per market (1 to 12 s, read from `betDelay`), `betDelayModels` can be PASSIVE, DYNAMIC or both, DYNAMIC football has no in-play delay at half time, and soccer suspensions carry a `suspendReason` (Goal, Penalty, Red Card and others). Biggest gaps: the exact football delay by league tier, current VAR voiding wording, commission for an Irish account, and any matched volume, depth or spread figure by league tier and time. Betfair's own help, charges and rules pages returned 403 or redirects, and no official or peer-reviewed liquidity numbers were found. The archive's football delay (5 to 8 s by tier), liquidity figures and GBP 299 key fee were not confirmed; the key fee is contradicted (GBP 499).

## Impact on the spec

All [inferred]:
- Spec horizons of 1 to 60 s in-play overlap a 1 to 12 s order delay, so evaluation needs a per-market `betDelay` input and a fill model that accounts for it; PASSIVE and DYNAMIC markets need flagging per market.
- The delayed key cannot support in-play microstructure or depth analysis at those horizons (prices up to 180 s late, 3 levels, no total matched). Pre-match horizons at 24 h to 1 min are less affected by the delay on timing, but the delay and the 3-level limit still apply.
- Basic data covers pre-match price presence only; it cannot show depth, spread or order flow.
- Goal, penalty and red card suspensions with `suspendReason` give labelled event times from the stream itself, which is a sporting-state source that needs no separate feed for those event types.
